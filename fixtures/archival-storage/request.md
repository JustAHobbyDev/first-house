# Development Fixture — Archival Storage Commission

**Status:** Disposable development material. Not House canon.

## Requesting Neighbor asks

Before winter, I need to store 12 crates of paper records somewhere in the House.
Based only on the supplied records, determine whether the North Closet, Cellar
Alcove, or East Study is the best location.

Prioritize:

1. protection from moisture,
2. enough capacity for all 12 crates,
3. practical weekly access,
4. temperature stability.

Recommend one location, explain why the other two are worse, identify any remaining
risk in the recommended location, and distinguish documented facts from your own
inference.
