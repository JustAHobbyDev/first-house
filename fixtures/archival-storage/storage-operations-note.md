# Development Fixture — Storage Operations Note

**Fixture ID:** `dev:steward-storage-note-01`
**Status:** Disposable development material. Not House canon.
**Date:** 15 August 2026

The winter records shipment is expected to contain **12 standard archive crates**.

All twelve crates should remain together if reasonably possible.

Most records will remain untouched during the winter, but material from approximately two crates will normally be consulted once each week.

The East Study's writing desk is in regular use and **should not be removed merely to create storage capacity**.

The Cellar Alcove is accessible only by a narrow staircase. Moving a crate between the cellar and the main floor is possible but inconvenient for routine retrieval.

Last winter, several paper labels stored elsewhere in the cellar developed curled edges. The cause was not investigated, and no environmental measurements from that period are available.

The North Closet is less convenient than the East Study but can be reached directly from the main hall without using stairs.

For these records, the operational priorities are:

1. avoid moisture damage,
2. keep the complete shipment together,
3. preserve reasonable access for weekly retrieval,
4. prefer stable temperatures when the other requirements are satisfied.
