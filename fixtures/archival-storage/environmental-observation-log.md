# Development Fixture — Environmental Observation Log

**Fixture ID:** `dev:environmental-observation-log-01`
**Status:** Disposable development material. Not House canon.
**Observation period:** 27 July–10 August 2026

Portable temperature and humidity loggers were placed in three possible storage locations.

Measurements below summarize the observation period.

| Location      | Temperature | Relative Humidity |
| ------------- | ----------: | ----------------: |
| North Closet  |     18–22°C |   normally 47–58% |
| Cellar Alcove |     15–18°C |            61–74% |
| East Study    |     19–27°C |            38–51% |

## North Closet

Humidity remained below 60% for nearly the entire observation period.

Following a heavy storm on 2 August, relative humidity briefly reached **71%**. The elevated reading lasted approximately six hours and returned below 60% within eight hours.

No liquid water was observed.

The cause of the temporary increase was not established.

## Cellar Alcove

Humidity exceeded 70% during portions of three separate days.

No standing water was observed during the logging period.

The cellar showed the smallest temperature variation of the three locations.

## East Study

The study remained the driest location.

Temperature increased substantially on sunny afternoons and occasionally reached 27°C before falling again in the evening.

No unusual humidity events were recorded.
