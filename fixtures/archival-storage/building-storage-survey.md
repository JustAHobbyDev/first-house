# Development Fixture — Building Storage Survey

**Fixture ID:** `dev:building-storage-survey-01`
**Status:** Disposable development material. Not House canon.
**Survey date:** 12 August 2026

Three spaces were inspected for possible storage of boxed paper records.

## North Closet

Interior usable floor area is approximately 2.1 m × 1.6 m. With the center passage kept clear, the closet can hold approximately **14 standard archive crates**.

Walls, ceiling, and floor were dry at the time of inspection. No staining, mold, or active water intrusion was observed.

The closet is inside the heated portion of the building but has no dedicated radiator. Observed temperature during the inspection was 21°C. Based on adjacent-room measurements, temperatures are expected to remain roughly between **18°C and 22°C** during normal occupancy.

The doorway has a 3 cm wooden threshold. Crates must be carried approximately 11 m from the main hall.

## Cellar Alcove

The alcove can hold approximately **24 standard archive crates**.

The cellar is cool and experiences relatively little daily temperature variation. During inspection, the alcove measured 17°C.

White mineral deposits were visible along the bottom of the west wall. A faint horizontal water mark approximately 4 cm above the floor was also visible.

Maintenance records supplied to the inspector state that drainage work was completed in May after water entered the cellar during an April storm. No active water was present during this inspection.

## East Study

With its existing furniture retained, the study has clear storage space for approximately **10 standard archive crates**.

Removing the writing desk would increase capacity to approximately 16 crates.

No signs of moisture intrusion were observed. The room receives direct afternoon sunlight through two east-facing windows and becomes noticeably warmer during clear weather.

The study is an occupied working room and is entered frequently throughout the day.
