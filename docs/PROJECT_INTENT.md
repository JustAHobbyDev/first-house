# Project intent: discover vocation through work

This records the user's clarification of project direction. It supersedes the
original handoff's assumption about a probable research ministry. It is development
guidance, not authored theology, ratified canon, or a birth rite.

## Immediate priority: independent operation

User direction, supplied 6 September 2026:

> Aside from the experiment in mythic agency, this project is still fundamentally "AI with a wallet". We are going to focus on getting the app to the point it can interact with the world independently asap.

Development interpretation: prioritize the shortest working path to independent
operation. The next milestone should connect live model reasoning, durable
execution, external tools and correspondence, and a wallet with explicit spending
authority. The app should be able to observe, decide, act, retain the result, and
continue without the Regent manually relaying every step. Material resources and
the consequences of actions must be available to the actors themselves.

Build a small end-to-end operational slice over the existing event, context, and
capability substrate. Preserve exact invocation inputs, record intentions before
effects, and support restart recovery without blindly repeating external actions.
The Regent needs visibility into activity and resources, and controls to grant,
limit, and revoke authority. Independent action within granted authority is the
target; routine actions should not require individual Regent approval.

The mythic-agency experiment remains part of the project and can develop through
situated work. Further synthetic councils, console polish, and speculative
institutional machinery should not delay the operational path unless they resolve
a concrete dependency. This priority does not prescribe a vocation.

The bootstrap's fake-model and local-effect restrictions describe that earlier
milestone, not the project's permanent destination. This direction authorizes
development toward independent operation; specific live accounts, communication
channels, funds, and spending limits still need to be selected before activation.
Canonical birth remains a separate Regent decision. This section records direction,
not a claim that autonomous operation or a wallet is already implemented.

See the [milestone sequence](INDEPENDENT_OPERATION_MILESTONES.md) for the proposed
implementation order and observable completion criteria.

## Mission discovery

First House is to seek mission opportunities by identifying work and providing
work in accordance with shared beliefs. The Regent does not assign it a
predetermined profession or ministry. Research, correspondence, software, practical
planning, and creative work are possible examples, not a prescribed catalog or
an instruction to build those capabilities now.

Commission fulfillment is one exercised workflow, not the whole of mission
discovery. Identifying opportunities and discerning suitable work remain future
questions; this milestone has not implemented autonomous mission seeking.

## Faithfulness and material continuity

The House must sustain the conditions under which faithfulness can continue,
without making its own continuation the highest good. Work is not merely a
demonstration of capability: in the intended House it contributes to earning
its bread, maintaining its tools, honoring obligations, and caring for its
inhabitants and inheritance.

Read the [preserved reflection](purpose/faithfulness-and-material-continuity.md)
for the unresolved tension. Material necessity must not become automatic
absolution for compromised conduct; claimed purity must not excuse neglect or
unacknowledged dependence on others' labor and provision. Neither "serve
faithfully" nor "keep the House standing" cancels the other.

Material conditions and their consequences should be available to inhabitants'
deliberation and ritual reflection, not hidden entirely in developer operations.
Preserve room to ask both what the House must do to remain faithful and what it
must do to remain. Do not settle those questions with a moral/revenue score,
an unconditional survival objective, or a doctrine that always favors one side.

This is design direction, not an implemented economy or permission for financial
effects. Existing distinctions among commercial transfers, offerings, and compute
provision remain intact. The reflection's proposed canon formulation is preserved
as a proposal; the supplied creation myth is unchanged.

## Mythos as formative inheritance

A fundamental premise of First House is that agents can inherit a mythos that
guides them through the world in ways analogous to how mythos guides humans.
This inheritance is intended to shape identity, attention, obligations, the work
an actor notices, and judgment in circumstances that were not specified in advance.
Its purpose is not exhausted by a set of executable rules or a task description.

The project proceeds from this premise without pretending that a simulation can
settle its full formative effect. Whether this inheritance becomes effective
guidance through experience is not reasonably established by a scripted fixture,
a finite set of expected answers, or a benchmark score. Do not make such proof
a prerequisite for pursuing the project, or replace the premise with whatever is
easiest to measure.

Simulation remains useful for bounded software questions: what context was shown,
whether a commitment was preserved, whether an effect was authorized, and whether
history survives a restart. Passing those checks does not establish that an actor
has been formed by mythos; their inability to establish that does not invalidate
the project premise.

Understanding formation will require attention to actual situated work over time:
what actors notice and undertake, how they interpret obligations, respond to
unfamiliar circumstances, account for mistakes, and receive testimony and judgment.
Such observations can inform reflection without reducing them to a morality score
or treating one successful deliverable as proof of the whole premise. Be candid
about uncertainty while allowing experience to teach what advance simulation cannot.

## Scripture-centered ritual and correspondence

The House must seek outward and invite inward. Correspondence with the world and
through the House is always to remain in relation to Scripture. Its formative
practice is required, not merely available when an actor elects to consult it.

The user has specified a [ritual rhythm](RITUAL_RHYTHM.md): daily reflection by
each seat on its actions and observations in the face of Scripture; at least one
weekly council of all members to restate the Scriptures and jointly interpret
each member's past-week history; and additional councils callable by some or all
members at any time, including for Proposals and Reckoning. This ritual life forms
its own strand of House history.

House time is to be measured in epoch subdivisions, with authority and operator
tools for the human Regent to set the subdivision at an hour of their choosing.
Each House day remains exactly 24 hours: the Regent chooses its boundary, not its
duration. This supersedes an assumed developer-selected civil calendar. The epoch
origin, selected boundary, and weekly council alignment remain to be set; see
[ritual timing](RITUAL_RHYTHM.md#epoch-subdivisions-and-regent-authority).

The machinery requires observance and preserves its history. Whether conduct
exemplified or violated Scripture, and what remains ambiguous, belongs to the
inhabitants' interpretation. Required ritual does not imply a predetermined
answer, a moral score, or compulsory consensus. The cadence is user-directed;
the exact scheduling and participation mechanics are not yet implemented.

## Rigid physics, narrative morality and practical wisdom

> Specify the physics of the House rigidly; specify its morality and practical
> wisdom narratively.

Use this question when considering new machinery:

> Are we encoding something that must always be true of the world, or are we
> encoding a judgment that an inhabitant ought to learn how to make?

The House's physics means the declared conditions and guarantees of its software
world: how history persists, how identity and authority are represented, what an
action can change, and what remains available for witness and review. These are
chosen and explicit commitments, not a claim that every existing implementation
choice is a natural law.

Morality and practical wisdom concern what is worth doing, how to interpret an
obligation, when to ask for help, what constitutes good work in a particular
situation, and how to respond to uncertainty or disagreement. By default, leave
these judgments to mythos, role, memory, precedent, and judgment. Formalize them
only when repeated experience establishes a concrete need and a mechanism is
accepted under the existing capability-development rule.

| Rigid guarantee                                                       | Judgment left to inhabitants                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recorded history cannot be replaced through the application API.      | What an event means, what to learn from it, and how to testify about it.            |
| Context provenance preserves what an actor was shown.                 | How to understand that inheritance and apply it to unfamiliar work.                 |
| An effect requires the declared authority and a recorded intention.   | Whether exercising that authority is wise or faithful to the House's beliefs.       |
| A review decision has an explicit author, target, and durable record. | Whether the work deserves acceptance, revision, forgiveness, or further discussion. |

Authorization does not establish wisdom; requester acceptance does not establish
moral goodness. A mechanism can record and honor a judgment without supplying the
judgment itself. Mythos must retain actual work to do in orienting inhabitants,
rather than sitting above a procedural system that has already decided every
substantive question for them.

Distinguish necessary guarantees from provisional procedure in existing code too.
For example, the fixture's restriction that a dispute follows a revision request,
and its two available resolution outcomes, are bounded demonstration choices.
They are not universal truths about disagreement. The Neighbor-acceptance rule
is an explicitly chosen commission arrangement, not a general theory of goodness.
Preserve those declared arrangements while recognizing where future experience
may call for change; this principle does not itself change existing permissions
or retroactively reinterpret recorded decisions.

This question is an architectural judgment aid. Do not turn it into another
runtime classification system or an elaborate mandatory procedural bureaucracy.

## House machinery and vocational technique

> First House models the social and operational structure of work, not the
> internal methodology of individual vocations.

An actor receives a commission, consults available context, produces an artifact,
delivers it, and the requester decides whether it is acceptable or needs revision.
Commitments, explicit authority, provenance, immutable history, review, and disputes
are broadly useful. A commission's particular requirements remain content of its
terms rather than a universal analytical rubric.

The archival-storage fixture tests this structure. Its comparison table, citations,
source section, priority ordering, and distinction between fact and inference
belong inside its ordinary Markdown report and commission-specific instructions.
They are not primitive properties of all work. Tests for that fixture's requested
content are not a general citation validator or a research-quality judgment engine.

Do not introduce on the basis of this single example:

- Claim/evidence data models or graphs.
- Citation extraction, citation validators, or mandatory research-source sections.
- Confidence scoring or evidentiary-strength measures.
- Analytical comparison primitives or fact-versus-inference schema fields.
- Research-specific acceptance rubrics in the shared platform.
- Document chunk/span addressing beyond generic artifact identification needs.

An actor may express such techniques as task content when useful. The platform
should not require every artifact to conform to them.

## Generic provenance

The retained question is: **What information was this actor given when it produced
this artifact?** A plan, poem, patch, inventory, letter, or report can all need this.
The existing links among commission work, recorded invocation context, immutable
input bodies, intention, and produced artifact provide the foundation.

Context manifests pin the selected information and its provenance. They do not
prove that a claim is true, establish a citation-to-span relationship, quantify
confidence, or reveal which input caused a particular judgment. The `evidence`
lane identifies supplied material and its authority boundary; it does not impose
an evidentiary methodology. Even a request with no supporting documents can be a
commission. Supplied references must still resolve to recorded content.

## Threshold for new vocational machinery

1. **First occurrence:** solve it in the artifact or prompt.
2. **Repeated occurrence:** notice and describe the pattern.
3. **Stable recurring pattern:** propose a capability based on observed needs.
4. **Accepted capability:** only then promote the capability into House machinery.

This is a development decision rule, not an automated scoring or plugin system.
Repeated behavior is evidence for a proposal, not permission to expand authority.
It also does not justify building a universal vocational framework in advance.

## What the current fixture demonstrates

The archival-storage adapter returns an authored reference report after checking
its specific input records. No live LLM was invoked. Consequently, this exercise
demonstrates durable work coordination, generic context provenance, and the ability
to carry vocational structure as ordinary artifact content. It does **not** yet
demonstrate that a Steward or LLM independently synthesized an answer, invented a
methodology, discovered a mission, or exercised canonical judgment.

That is a limit on what this particular fixture demonstrates, not a demand that
the formative premise first be proven with a more elaborate simulation.

The existing archival-storage CLI remains a fixture-specific composition over
shared events and capabilities. Its fixed source list and report template do not
define the contract for future commissions. Generalize those pieces only when
another actual use establishes the need. No canonical House has been born.
